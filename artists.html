Artists & Gallery

Discover the artists and creative categories featured at the festival - from paintings and photography to crafts, illustration, and safe body art demonstrations.

Featured Categories

These categories help visitors find what they’re most interested in.

Paintings

Traditional and modern painting styles - landscapes, portraits, abstract work, and community-themed pieces.

  • Pop-up gallery displays
  • Live painting demos
  • Featured local artists

Photography

Photography showcasing Burnley, local life, and creative storytelling through portraits and scenes.

  • Town & community themes
  • Student showcase area
  • Print display wall

Illustration & Design

Posters, digital art, illustration and graphic design - including community projects and commissions.

  • Digital + traditional
  • Local designer corner
  • Sketchbook table

Body Art

Face painting for families plus body art demonstrations using safe materials and hygiene rules.

  • Family zone sessions
  • Artist-led designs
  • Bookable slots at busy times

Featured Artists

Some of the creative work you’ll see across the weekend.

Amira Khan - Acrylic Painter

Amira creates bold acrylic pieces inspired by Lancashire landscapes and city textures.

  • Style: Acrylic, modern landscapes
  • Where to find: Pop-up Gallery (Zone A)
  • Demo: Saturday 14:00

Tom Barlow - Street Photographer

Tom documents everyday life in Burnley, capturing candid moments and portraits that tell small stories.

  • Style: Documentary photography
  • Where to find: Photo Wall (Zone B)
  • Talk: Sunday 13:00

Riya Patel - Illustrator

Riya makes character illustration and poster-style art, mixing digital and traditional techniques.

  • Style: Digital illustration + ink
  • Where to find: Design Corner (Zone C)
  • Workshop: Sunday 11:30

Jay & Crew - Body Art Demonstrations

A small team demonstrating safe body art techniques and face painting designs with proper hygiene standards.

  • Style: Face painting + body art
  • Where to find: Family Zone (Zone D)
  • Sessions: Both days (bookable)

  • Clear hygiene rules
