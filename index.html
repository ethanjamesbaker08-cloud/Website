Plan Your Visit

Key information for visitors.

Getting Here

The festival takes place around Burnley town centre with pop-up venues within walking distance. On the day, signage and volunteers help visitors find zones and stages.

  • Arrive early for the parade
  • Comfortable shoes recommended
  • Busiest time: mid-afternoon

Accessibility & Safety

We aim to keep routes clear and information easy to read. Seating areas and a quiet space are available for visitors who need a break.

  • Clearly marked zones
  • Family-friendly activities
  • Safe materials for face/body art

FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

Is the festival free?

Many areas are free to enter. Some workshops may have limited tickets or small fees to cover materials.

Can I apply to perform or run a stall?

Yes - applications are on the Get Involved page.

What kinds of art are included?

Paintings, illustration, photography, community art, dance, parade performances, street performance, and body art.
