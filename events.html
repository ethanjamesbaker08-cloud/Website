Upcoming Events

Browse the weekend schedule and pick what you want to see - parade routes, dance showcases, live painting, food stalls, street performers, and safe body art sessions.

How to Use the Schedule

The festival is split into zones. Some events repeat throughout the day, while others happen at set times. If you’re unsure where to go, start at the Info Point and follow the signs.

  • Main Stage: bigger showcases (dance / music / highlights)
  • High Street: roaming acts and street performers
  • Pop-up Gallery: exhibitions and live painting
  • Family Zone: face painting and family activities

Workshops & Sessions

Some sessions are limited capacity, especially face painting and body art. Slots can be booked on the day or sometimes ticketed to cover materials.

  • Arrive early for popular sessions
  • Ask volunteers about waiting times
  • Safe materials + hygiene rules

Opening Parade

A community-led parade with music, banners, costumes, and performers. Best viewing spots are along the main route.

  • Good for photos
  • Arrive early for front spots
  • Volunteers guide the route

Dance Showcase

Short, high-energy sets throughout the day, with a bigger slot on the main stage.

  • Main stage + roaming sets
  • Easy to dip in/out
  • Family-friendly

Live Painting

Watch artists create work live and hear how they do it. Finished pieces may be displayed in the pop-up gallery.

  • Great for art students
  • Q&A after the demo
  • Calmer zone if it’s busy outside

Face Painting / Body Art

Safe materials and hygiene rules, with sessions organised so queues stay manageable.

  • Bookable slots at peak times
  • Family zone support
  • Artist-led designs
