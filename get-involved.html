Roles We Need

A quick overview of the kind of help we’re looking for.

Volunteer Roles

  • Info point: directions, maps, basic questions
  • Queue support: family zone / face painting sessions
  • Stage support: timekeeping + helping performers
  • Floating helper: general support across zones

Volunteers get a badge and a short briefing.

Performer / Stall Notes

  • Performers: arrive 30 minutes early for sound checks
  • Street acts: keep walkways clear and follow staff directions
  • Stalls: list power needs and setup time in your application
  • Body art: safe materials + hygiene rules required

It keeps things running smoothly on the day.

FAQ

Common questions from volunteers, performers, and stallholders.

Do I need experience to volunteer?

No - roles are straightforward and you’ll get a quick briefing. We’ll match tasks to what you’re comfortable with.

Can students apply to perform or exhibit?

Yes. We welcome new and local talent - just describe what you do and what you need.

Are face painting and body art sessions safe?

Yes - safe materials + hygiene rules, and sessions are managed so it doesn’t get overcrowded.

How do stall spaces work?

Spaces are limited. We confirm size, setup time, and any power needs once we’ve reviewed applications.
