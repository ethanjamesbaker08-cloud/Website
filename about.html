About Us

Burnley Community Arts Festival is a new annual, non-profit event built to showcase local creativity and make the arts feel welcoming, visible, and easy to take part in.

Our Mission

Our goal is to create a festival that celebrates all forms of creativity - from traditional paintings and gallery work to street performances, dance, and body art. We want visitors to discover local talent and feel comfortable taking part, even if they’ve never been to an arts event before.

  • Promote local artists and performers
  • Create a friendly, accessible event for visitors
  • Support community groups and young creatives
  • Keep the event affordable as a non-profit

Why We Started

Burnley has loads of creative talent, but it’s not always easy for people to find it. The festival was created to bring everything together in one place: artists, performers, food stalls, and family activities all sharing the same space across a weekend.

Each year we build on what worked well and make improvements based on feedback from visitors and artists.

Our Values

These are the things we try to stick to when planning the festival.

Community First

We work with local artists, schools, and community groups so the festival feels properly Burnley-led.

Welcoming & Inclusive

Clear info, friendly volunteers, and spaces that feel comfortable to explore.

Creative Variety

From paintings to dance to body art, we give different art forms space to shine.

Safe & Responsible

Face painting and body art use safe materials and hygiene rules so families can join in confidently.

How the Festival Works

What to expect, whether you’re visiting or taking part.

For Visitors

Most areas are open and free to explore. You can browse exhibitions, watch performances, and visit stalls. Some workshops are ticketed or limited to keep sessions organised and cover materials.

  • Look for zone signage and maps
  • Check times on the Events page
  • Ask volunteers if you need help

For Artists & Performers

We offer slots for performers, space for exhibitions, and opportunities for live demos. We also welcome new artists who want to be part of the festival, including younger creatives and first-time performers.

  • Artist showcases and pop-up galleries
  • Street performance areas
  • Workshops and demonstrations

Organisers & Support

As a non-profit festival, support from the community makes a big difference.

Who runs it?

The festival is organised by a small volunteer-led team supported by local partners. Roles include planning the schedule, coordinating venues, supporting artists, and managing volunteer teams on the day.

How you can support

Support can be time, talent, or resources. Volunteering helps keep the festival running smoothly and keeps costs down for visitors and artists.

  • Volunteer shifts (meet & greet / info desk)
  • Donations and local sponsorship
  • Sharing the event with friends
